Frequently Asked Questions

What photos do I need to send?

Just the ones already on your Airbnb or Instagram listing. We work with standard property photos, no professional photography required. The more rooms and angles you have, the better the final video.

How long does delivery take?

Single clips are delivered within 3 business days, clip packs within 5 to 7 business days, and full walkthrough videos within 5 business days. Rush delivery is available on request.

What if I want changes?

The Full Walkthrough package includes 1 revision round. Additional revisions can be arranged for a small fee. Single clips are delivered as-is, but we always aim to nail it on the first try.

Can I use the video on multiple platforms?

Absolutely. You own the video and can use it on Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO, Instagram, TikTok, your personal website, anywhere you want.

Do you offer discounts for multiple properties?

Yes! If you manage multiple listings, reach out and we'll create a custom package for you.
